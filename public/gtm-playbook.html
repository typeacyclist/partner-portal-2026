Core Value Proposition

Trendzact helps organizations control sensitive data exposure the moment it becomes visible—across screens, people, workspaces, meetings, behavior, and evidence.

Sales Narrative

1. Data is protected in systems

Security investments focus on storage, transmission, and classification.

2. Exposure begins when data is visible

The moment sensitive data appears on screen, it enters the human and physical world.

3. Existing tools miss this moment

DLP, UEBA, and logs do not fully control what is visible, who is present, or the surrounding context.

4. Trendzact controls the moment

Trendzact turns exposure into a real-time control point using visibility, presence, and context.

Solution Mapping

Workspace Exposure

Remote work, phones, observers, and unattended screens create physical exposure risk.

Virtual Meeting & Screenshare Exposure

Sensitive data is shared in meetings with external participants and recordings.

Imposters & Identity Spoofing

Valid logins do not prove the correct person is present during sensitive work.

On-Screen Sensitive Data Exposure

DLP does not control what users actually see on screen.

Insider Risk & Exfiltration Behavior

Behavioral patterns indicate risk before data leaves the organization.

Evidence, Audit & Reconstruction

Organizations need proof of what was visible, who was present, and what happened.

Discovery Questions

Use these to identify which exposure coverage to lead with.

Exposure Risk

  • Where is sensitive data most exposed once it is visible?
  • What happens when sensitive data is shared in meetings?
  • Can you confirm who is present during sensitive work?
  • Do you have proof of what was visible during an incident?

Current Controls

  • What happens after a risky event is detected?
  • How are sensitivity labels enforced at the screen level?
  • How long does it take to investigate insider-related alerts?
  • How complete is your evidence for audit or incident review?

Objection Handling

"This feels like surveillance."

Trendzact uses privacy-aware, policy-driven controls. Evidence is captured only when exposure risk triggers action.

"We already have DLP or Purview."

Trendzact extends those investments by controlling the moment data becomes visible—not just when it moves.

"Our SOC cannot handle more alerts."

Trendzact reduces noise by linking exposure, identity, and behavior into high-confidence events.

"Can’t meeting platforms do this?"

Collaboration tools provide basic controls. Trendzact enforces sensitivity-aware protection during exposure moments.
