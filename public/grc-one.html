GRC1 Definition

11 Categories of Data Exposure

All exposure shares one characteristic: it occurs at the moment data is visible — not when it is transferred.

01

Visual Exposure (What is visible)

  • Sensitive data displayed on screen in open environments
  • Financial, PHI, or IP visible during screen sharing
  • Multi-monitor setups exposing different data simultaneously
  • Documents or dashboards left open and unattended
  • Screens visible through glass walls, public seating, or travel
02

Unauthorized Presence (Who is present)

  • Shoulder surfing in office or public locations
  • Unknown individuals visible in camera frame during work
  • Family members or bystanders viewing remote work screens
  • External participants in meetings viewing restricted data
  • Session takeover (user leaves, another person continues work)
03

Environmental Exposure (Where it occurs)

  • Remote work in uncontrolled environments (cafes, airports, hotels)
  • Shared workspaces or hot-desking without controls
  • Sensitive work conducted during travel
  • External meeting environments with unknown participants
  • Use of unmanaged or insecure physical locations
04

Behavioral Exposure (How it happens)

  • Screenshots of sensitive data
  • Screen recordings during workflows or meetings
  • Copy/paste (clipboard) of sensitive information to other apps
  • Sharing data via personal email, chat, or cloud storage
  • Opening multiple apps to move or replicate data
  • Rapid switching between sensitive and non-sensitive contexts
05

Collaboration-Based Exposure

  • Sensitive data shared in virtual meetings
  • Unauthorized attendees joining calls
  • Screen sharing without awareness of audience
  • Recording or capturing meeting content
  • Chat-based sharing of restricted information
06

Multi-Channel / Multi-Surface Exposure

  • Data visible on one screen and captured on another
  • Secondary monitor used to bypass controls
  • Split workflows across devices or windows
  • Simultaneous use of secure and insecure apps
07

Shadow Capture (Off-System Exposure)

  • Phone camera capturing screens or documents
  • Personal devices used to photograph sensitive data
  • External recording tools not managed by IT
  • Printed material photographed or scanned
08

Identity-Related Exposure

  • Credential sharing between employees
  • Unauthorized person using an active session
  • Identity not verified during sensitive actions
  • Multiple individuals interacting with the same session
09

Privileged / High-Risk Exposure

  • Admin viewing or exporting sensitive datasets
  • Engineering access to IP or source code
  • Finance/legal reviewing confidential transactions
  • Executive sessions involving M&A or strategy
10

Timing & Contextual Exposure (When it matters)

  • Exposure during high-risk workflows (e.g., data export, reporting)
  • Late-night or unusual access patterns
  • Exposure during role transitions (resignations, layoffs)
  • Repeated exposure events over time (pattern-based risk)
11

Evidence & Accountability Gaps

  • No record of what data was visible
  • No proof of who was present
  • No timeline of exposure events
  • Inability to demonstrate control during audits

How This Ties to GRC1

All eleven exposure categories share a common characteristic: they occur at the moment data is visible, not when it is transferred.

GRC1 Addresses These By:

  • Understanding exposure across all vectors simultaneously
  • Evaluating risk in real time
  • Taking action during the exposure moment
